Griddle Process:
1.) Arm is positioned near the edge of the pancake
2.) Arm slides under the pancake 
3.) Arm lifts pancake and move to left side of griddle
4.) Arm flips using a 
controlled wrist rotation
5.) Arm returns to the right side of the griddle 
6.) Arm performs a sweeping motion across the griddle 
7.) Arm lifts the pancake and moves over to plate

SLIDE 18
Flipping the Pancake over Plate
Plate/Toppings Process:
Arm is positioned on the edge of the plate
Arm flips pancake 
Arm returns to edge of plate position
Flipping Technique:
Slow rotation of J5 joint, simulating wrist rotation
Flipping motion works at 15% speed ensuring priority for accuracy over speed
This flipping technique uses less torque which allows for larger pancakes to be flipped

Our main goal with this project was to produce a reliable semi-automated pancake flipping robot that could produce accurate and repeatable results
